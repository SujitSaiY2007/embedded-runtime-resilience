# References Used — Embedded Runtime Resilience

This folder consolidates the references and source leads documented during the project's research and prior-art investigation. It is intended to support a future manuscript and make the research trail auditable.

## Files

- [Reference register](REFERENCE_REGISTER.md) — unique source inventory, source type, relevance, and verification status.
- [Counting and verification notes](COUNTING_AND_VERIFICATION.md) — how to interpret the inventory and why the current count is not a claim about every source encountered during the month.

## Important scope rule

This is a **reconstruction from the canonical repository's available research reports and checkpoint records**, not proof that every item was read in full or cited in a manuscript. The register distinguishes primary-source inspection from metadata/abstract-only review, candidate leads, and references whose exact bibliographic details still require verification. Do not cite an item in a manuscript solely because it appears here.

The repository's K.10.1–K.10.4 documents are technical prior-art analyses, not legal opinions. Their bounded findings must remain bounded. A reference's presence here does not establish that it anticipates the complete P4 design, nor that P4 is novel or patentable.

## Maintenance

When adding a source, record its full bibliographic details, stable URL/DOI or patent identifier, source type, what was actually inspected, and where it is used in the manuscript. Deduplicate by DOI, patent publication/family, or canonical source URL where appropriate. Keep candidate leads separate from verified references.
